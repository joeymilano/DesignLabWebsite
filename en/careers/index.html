Apply as an engineering collaborator↗

What you will do

  • Build staged learning and project plans around each learner's background, target discipline and school requirements
  • Guide research, concepts, experiments, making, narrative and portfolio editing while preserving full student authorship
  • Lead one-to-one or group critiques with actionable feedback, clear progress records and stronger student articulation

What matters

  • A postgraduate degree from a strong international art or design program, or equivalent teaching and industry practice
  • Current knowledge of international portfolio expectations and the ability to turn critique into concrete next steps
  • Reliable remote teaching and the ability to mentor clearly in Mandarin or fluent English
